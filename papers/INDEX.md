# Source papers

These are the source artifacts for the first four trial notebooks. The PDFs were
retrieved from the PMC article viewer through Europe PMC/PMC and validated as
PDFs with `pypdf` (page counts below). NICE-SUGAR predates the PMC author-
manuscript deposit available for the other trials, so it has a source note
instead of a copied paywalled PDF.

| Notebook | Trial paper | Registration | Open source artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| `nice-sugar.py` | NICE-SUGAR Study Investigators. *Intensive versus Conventional Glucose Control in Critically Ill Patients.* NEJM 2009;360:1283–1297. DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa0810625](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0810625). | NCT00220987 | [`nice-sugar-2009-SOURCE-NOTE.md`](nice-sugar-2009-SOURCE-NOTE.md) |
| `recovery-dexamethasone.py` | RECOVERY Collaborative Group. *Dexamethasone in Hospitalized Patients with Covid-19.* NEJM 2020;384:693–704. DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa2021436](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2021436). | NCT04381936; ISRCTN50189673 | [`recovery-dexamethasone-2021.pdf`](recovery-dexamethasone-2021.pdf), 11 pages |
| `sprint.py` | SPRINT Research Group. *A Randomized Trial of Intensive versus Standard Blood-Pressure Control.* NEJM 2015;373:2103–2116. DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa1511939](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1511939). | NCT01206062 | [`sprint-2015.pdf`](sprint-2015.pdf), 22 pages |
| `ischemia.py` | ISCHEMIA Research Group. *Initial Invasive or Conservative Strategy for Stable Coronary Disease.* NEJM 2020;382:1395–1407. DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa1915922](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1915922). | NCT01471522 | [`ischemia-2020.pdf`](ischemia-2020.pdf), 25 pages |

## Retrieval notes

- RECOVERY: PMCID [PMC7383595](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7383595/).
- SPRINT: PMCID [PMC4689591](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4689591/).
- ISCHEMIA: PMCID [PMC7263833](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7263833/).
- NICE-SUGAR: Europe PMC abstract [PMID 19318384](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/19318384) plus ClinicalTrials.gov [NCT00220987](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00220987); see its source note for the complete provenance boundary.

The PDF files are retained as research inputs for local notebook generation;
check the publication and repository terms before redistributing them.
