# NICE-SUGAR (2009) — source note

**Trial:** Intensive versus Conventional Glucose Control in Critically Ill Patients
**Citation:** The NICE-SUGAR Study Investigators (Finfer S, Chittock DR, Su SY, et al).
N Engl J Med 2009;360:1283-1297. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa0810625
**Registration:** NCT00220987
**PMID:** 19318384

## Why there is no PDF here

The NEJM 2009 article has no open-access deposit (not in PubMed Central; publisher paywall).
All figures in `notebooks/nice-sugar.py` are transcribed from:

1. The published abstract (Europe PMC: europepmc.org/article/MED/19318384) — flow counts,
   primary outcome OR 1.14 (95% CI 1.02-1.28), hypoglycaemia 206/3016 (6.8%) vs 15/3014 (0.5%),
   achieved means 115 vs 144 mg/dL, insulin use 97.2% vs 69.0%.
2. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT00220987 record — design, eligibility, targets.
3. The Bottom Line critical appraisal (thebottomline.org.uk/summaries/icm/nice-sugar/) —
   screening count (40,171), minimisation factors, sample-size assumption (90% power for 3.8%
   absolute difference at baseline mortality 30%).

Where the paper reported only percentages, denominators were reconstructed so arithmetic
reproduces published rates (e.g. 829/3010 = 27.5%). Each literal in the notebook carries this
provenance inline.

If you obtain the PDF legitimately, drop it here as `nice-sugar-2009.pdf` and re-verify.
